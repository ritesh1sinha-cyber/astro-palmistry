FAQ — Straight answers about AI-assisted palmistry and readings

  • I don't know my exact birth time. Is this useless to me?

    No — this is the case we built for. Palmistry needs no birth data. No birth time? Start with a question, or a photograph of your hands—both work today. Horary is cast from the moment you ask, and rectification reconstructs your birth time from life events you remember.

  • Is this just AI writing horoscopes?

    No. The astrology is computed — Swiss Ephemeris positions, classical rule engines, Ashtakavarga arithmetic, sixteen divisional charts, a computer-vision pass on your palm photographs. Every statement can be traced to a specific planet and house, which is why we print that alongside it. Language is polished for readability; the judgements are calculated.

  • Do you predict how long I will live?

    Never. The engines can compute it and we deliberately do not show it. Where a chart indicates a difficult health period you get the window and something practical to do about it. Nobody should learn that from software.

  • What if the reading doesn't match me?

    Tell us — there is a button. In most cases it means the birth time is off by enough to move your rising sign, which shifts every house and every prediction with it. We will route you to rectification, or to the two readings that need no birth data at all.

  • Why should I trust the accuracy?

    Don't, initially. Read what we say about your last six months and your past — both free, both about a life you have already lived. That is a test you can score immediately. Pay only if we pass it.
