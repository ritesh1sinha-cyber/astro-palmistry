A useful next step

Your birth time may need checking.

Even a small difference in birth time can change the ascendant and house positions, which can affect the timing of a reading. Hospital records often round to the nearest five or ten minutes, and families sometimes remember approximately. That does not prove the birth time is wrong — but it is the most common place to look when a present reading feels off.

Three ways forward:

  1. Birth-time rectification. Give us four or five dates from your life and we test hundreds of candidate minutes against them, then rebuild your chart on the one that fits. This is the real fix.
  2. Ask a question instead. A horary reading is cast from the moment you ask, so it needs no birth time at all. If your birth data is unreliable, this is the most accurate thing we can do for you.
  3. Read your palms. Needs no birth data whatsoever. Your hands are their own record.
