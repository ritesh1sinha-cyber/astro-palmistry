Then something upstream is wrong

Most likely, your birth time is not accurate.

This is the common case, not a rare one. Hospital records round to the nearest five or ten minutes, and families remember approximately. A twenty-minute error moves your rising sign. Once that moves, every house shifts, so every prediction built on it shifts with it. A reading that does not sound like you is usually a chart that is not quite yours.
